The two dissenting members, Zax and T'Vella, wore their reluctance like a uniform. Zax's posture was rigid, his shoulders squared in a way that was more defiant than disciplined. He kept his gaze fixed on a distant shelf, refusing to make eye contact with the levitating librarian, the indignity of the situation a palpable aura around him. T'Vella, for her part, was a picture of perfect, almost severe, Vulcan composure. Her movements were too precise, her expression too neutral. She was complying with a direct order from her captain, but her entire being radiated a quiet, logical protest against the intellectual impurity of their chosen method.

They took their two chosen books to the Alakazam librarian. He opened his eyes, and the team felt his psychic presence focus on them, a sensation like a powerful, active sensor sweep. Elara flinched slightly, feeling the immense, ancient mind brush against hers, not with any malice, but with a curiosity so profound it was overwhelming. The two books floated from their grasp, hovering in the air before the Alakazam. With a flick of his telekinetic will, the pages of both books began to riffle with impossible speed, a blur of motion that settled in an instant. The librarian had scanned their contents. A number, clear as a bell, echoed directly in each of their minds: Twenty Poké. Eva nodded, retrieving the small pouch of coins they had earned. She carefully counted out twenty of the strange, square coins, the unfamiliar weight and texture a constant reminder of their new reality. She placed them on the desk, and they floated into a small, waiting collection box.

As they stepped back out into the late afternoon, the sun casting long shadows across Havenwood, they felt a renewed sense of purpose. In their satchel, they carried two books: one holding the secrets to this world's unique science, the other holding the key to unlock it. The journey back to the Guildhall was quiet, each member lost in their own thoughts. Anya's mind was already deconstructing the diagrams from the enchantment tome, trying to reconcile the impossible physics with her own engineering knowledge. T'Vella was contemplating the most efficient methodology for learning the new language, her mind already building a lesson plan. Zax walked with a stiff, proud silence, his mind warring between the indignity of their new textbook and the tactical necessity of the mission. And Eva, carrying the weight of their collective hope and frustration, simply focused on the path ahead. Tonight, their education would begin.
