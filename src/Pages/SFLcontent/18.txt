"It also means we can't be chased," Kenji chirped, a note of relief in his voice. "That's good, right?"

"It is," Eva confirmed. "But it also means Cinder and Leaf are trapped down here with whatever is on the next floor. We can't afford to waste time." She opened the team satchel, taking inventory of their remaining supplies. "We're down to four Sitrus Berries. We need to be judicious."

They spent a few precious minutes in silence. Anya tightened the strap of Zax's Macho Brace where the Rock Slide had knocked it loose; he pretended not to wince. Kenji preened rust flakes out of his wings. T'Vella sat with her hands folded in her lap, very still, and Elara stayed close enough that their shoulders touched. The rest helped their bodies, but it also gave them time to think, and the cold dread came seeping back in.

"Let's move," Eva said finally, pushing herself to her paws. The break was over.

They proceeded down the only remaining path, a narrow tunnel that sloped ever deeper into the earth. The air grew colder, and the metallic tang of rust was stronger here. The tunnel opened into a new chamber. This room was different. The floor was a lattice of rusted metal grating over a dark, bottomless chasm. At its far edge, a steep metal ramp spiraled down along the chasm toward the next level. And waiting at the top of those stairs, as if guarding them, was a single Pokémon.

It was the Mawile that Rumble had warned them about. Its back was to them, but the large, carnivorous jaws that grew from its head swiveled to face them, a low, menacing growl building deep in its throat. One of its legs was bent at an unnatural angle, and a deep, ugly gash ran along its side, weeping a slow, dark fluid. It was injured, cornered, and looked ready to fight to the death to protect its position.

"Hostile contact!" Zax barked, taking a step forward to shield the others. "It's guarding the only way down."

"It's wounded," Elara noted, her voice tight with a mixture of pity and fear. "Rumble must have put up a good fight."

The Mawile didn't wait for them to strategize. Before Eva could even issue a command, it lunged. Not forward, but sideways, a blur of motion that seemed to defy its injured state. A shadowy energy enveloped it as it moved. "Sucker Punch!" Eva yelled, recognizing the priority attack. "Zax, brace!"

The Lucario crossed his arms just in time to take the brunt of the blow. The impact sent a jarring shockwave through him, and he skidded back a foot on the metal grating, the sound scraping loudly in the cavernous room. The Mawile, using the momentum of its attack, spun around, its massive jaws snapping viciously. With a roar, it brought the steel-hard appendage crashing down.

"Iron Head!" Zax growled, dropping low and pushing off the grating to meet the attack with a Force Palm. The clash of steel on aura energy rang out like a hammer on an anvil, a deafening clang that echoed into the chasm below. The force of the impact knocked both combatants back, the Mawile staggering on its injured leg, Zax landing gracefully but with a grimace of effort.

"It's strong..." Anya said, the flames on her neck flaring to life. "And desperate."

They had expected a moment's pause, a brief window to regroup and counter. They were wrong. The Mawile recovered with a speed that belied its injuries, a swiftness they hadn't seen in the more heavily armored steel-types on the floors above. It ignored the heavily-armored Zax and instead launched itself at Eva, its huge, secondary jaws gaping wide, crackling with dark energy.

"Captain!" Kenji shrieked.

The Mawile was blindingly fast. Eva's eyes widened, her combat instincts screaming. "Crunch!" she snarled, identifying the attack a split-second before it landed. She threw herself to the side, a burst of speed from a Quick Attack carrying her clear just as the monstrous jaws snapped shut with the force of a hydraulic press, the sound echoing like a gunshot in the metal room. The tips of the jaws grazed her flank, sending a jolt of icy pain through her, but the attack had missed its mark.

"It's faster than it looks!" Anya yelled. "We need to end this now!" She took the initiative, her body wreathed in a spinning Flame Wheel. She shot across the grating, a roaring vortex of fire aimed directly at the Mawile's injured side. The super-effective attack struck true, and the Mawile shrieked, the smell of scorched metal filling the air as it was thrown off balance.

The opening was all Kenji needed. "On your six!" he cried, a pilot's instinct taking over. He activated his Agility, becoming a silver blur that streaked past the staggering Mawile. He didn't strike on the first pass, but used the burst of speed to position himself perfectly. He came around in a tight, high-G turn, his wings glowing with a brilliant white light, and slashed across the Mawile's back with a powerful Wing Attack. The combined force of the fire and the aerial assault sent the creature stumbling forward, directly into T'Vella's line of sight.

"Target acquired," the Gardevoir stated, her voice a cold, calm whisper. Her eyes glowed with a violet light, and an unseen force seized the Mawile, lifting it a foot off the grating, its legs kicking uselessly. It was held fast, a perfect, helpless target.

"Execute," Eva commanded, the order sharp and absolute.

Zax, Eva, and Elara struck as one. Zax launched an Aura Sphere that slammed into the immobilized Mawile's chest, the energy detonating with concussive force. Eva's horn glowed with dark energy as she lunged, her Night Slash carving a deep, final wound. And Elara, seeing the creature's life force flickering, darted in, her Draining Kiss not an attack, but a final, merciful act, siphoning away the last of its pained energy and leaving only an empty husk.

The telekinetic grip vanished. The Mawile's body, now lifeless, fell to the metal grating with a final, hollow clang before dissolving into shimmering particles of light. The room was silent once more, save for the ragged breathing of the victorious team. The path to the fourth floor was clear.

A beat of silence, then a low chuckle from Zax broke the tension. "Combat efficiency: one hundred percent," he said, panting slightly. "Optimal."

Anya let out a puff of smoke, a grin on her face. "The power transfer was flawless. Like bypassing a faulty relay and routing it directly to the main phaser array. Beautiful."

"That was better than a perfect docking maneuver," Kenji chirped, landing on Eva's shoulder, his chest puffed out with pride. "Perfect formation flying!"

It was true. The fight hadn't been a desperate scramble for survival. It had been a symphony of controlled violence, a perfectly executed tactical maneuver where each member had played their part flawlessly. For the first time, it didn't feel like they were just using strange new powers; it felt like they were a Starfleet crew again, every officer executing their role with precision. The nature of this world, with its strange rules and spontaneous abilities, was beginning to feel less like a prison and more like a new, exciting set of operational parameters.

Eva looked at her crew, at their tired but exhilarated faces, and felt a surge of pride. "Excellent work, all of you," she said, her voice warm. "Let's get our friends and go home."

With renewed vigor, they began their descent. The path forward was not a tunnel, but a steep, spiraling ramp of dark metal that hugged the edge of the chasm. "This is... out of place," Anya muttered, her heavy tread causing the metallic ground to groan in protest. The surface wasn't smooth, but formed of interlocking crystalline plates that created a series of unnervingly regular ledges, almost like steps. The sound of their paws and claws on the metal came back at them in a close, rhythmic clang that felt claustrophobic despite the open air. "The geometric precision is... improbable," Zax added, his voice low. "Such formations do not typically occur in nature without an organizing principle." They arrived on the fourth floor, the main floor of the dungeon, and the sight that met them stole their breath and chilled them to the bone.

This was the heart of the decay. The cavern was immense, a silent cathedral of ruin where the air hissed with a biting cold. Massive, load-bearing pillars of natural iron ore, almost architectural in their formation, held up the ceiling. Their surfaces were thick with a blanket of orange corrosion, like dried blood on old bones, and the walls between them were a chaotic lattice of rock and rusted metal veins. The sheer scale of the dead space made the two small figures huddled in the center look terribly vulnerable. One was Cinder, the flame on his tail guttering low. The other was Leaf, curled into a protective ball. They were both covered in frost, their bodies trembling uncontrollably.

"Cinder! Leaf!" Eva cried, the team rushing forward.

"Stay... back..." Cinder chattered, his teeth clacking together. He looked up, his eyes wide with terror, and pointed a trembling claw towards the far end of the cavern. "It's... it's still here..."

Just as he spoke, a deep, grinding roar echoed from the darkness, a sound that seemed to make the very air crystallize. The ground trembled violently, and a colossal form smashed down from a massive fissure in the ceiling, landing directly in front of them with a deafening crash that sent shockwaves of frigid air and metal dust blasting through the cavern. It was another Steelix, but this one was a nightmare titan compared to its cousin from the Crystal Caves. It was larger, its segmented body plated in a dark, gunmetal grey alloy that seemed to absorb the dim light, and every inch of its form was scarred and pitted, signs of countless brutal victories. Its red eyes burned not with simple territorial rage, but with a cold, patient, malevolent intelligence. This was no mere guardian. This was the apex predator of this dungeon, the hunter that had broken Team Cinder, and it had been waiting for them.
