My Story

Hello! I'm a passionate and self-taught developer with a knack for problem-solving and a love for technology. My journey into the world of code began not in a classroom, but with a curiosity for how games worked. This led me to dive headfirst into LUA scripting, where I spent countless hours tweaking, building, and bringing my own ideas to life within game environments.

This hands-on experience taught me more than just syntax; it taught me how to think like a developer, how to debug complex systems, and how to collaborate with others to build something amazing. From there, my interests expanded. I found a similar joy in the tangible world of PC hardware—diagnosing issues, building machines from scratch, and optimizing performance. This blend of software and hardware knowledge gives me a unique, holistic perspective on technology.

Now, I'm channeling that same energy and dedication into web development, creating intuitive and beautiful user experiences. This portfolio is a showcase of my journey so far, from my early projects to my latest work. Feel free to explore!
