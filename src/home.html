Aeronautica

Administrative Tools / Backend Developer

Train, Buy, Fly - Aeronautica is an open-world flight simulator with economy elements where the goal is entirely up to you. Earn money by completing unique jobs or flights between distant islands and continents. You can even create an airline for your friends to join! Train and earn licenses to unlock new exciting vehicles. Customize and maintain your planes as you work your way up from the smallest Ultralights to the world’s largest Airliners like the Airbus A380 Superjumbo.

TTMP 2

Sole Developer - Passion Project

TTMP, or Tome Tales Management Panel is a program I developed to aid in the randomization of enemies, jobs and locations for some of the DnD games I played with my friends. Seeing as we wanted to introduce currency keeping and a whole lot more complicated systems that needed tracking, we eventually wanted a program that handled these things for us, or at least helped us in that regard. With that, the seed for this thing was born, a seed I'd happily cultivate into this handy little tool for us all. Quite the learning process too!
